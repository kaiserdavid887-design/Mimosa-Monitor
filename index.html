SCIENCE FAIR PROTOTYPE · v0.2

Plant & calibration

Real USB readings use your plant’s calibration. Demo readings use fixed artificial references.

Latest raw moisture reading: —

Use references from the same sensor and pot. They must differ by at least 50 ADC counts. The index is relative, not an absolute soil-water percentage.

Saving changes ends the current session and starts a fresh one if monitoring is running.

Start with the demo.

  1. Try a care notice. Click Dry soil and wait about 6 seconds. Click Water and wait about 3 seconds for a separate notice.
  2. Explore a signal. Signal example adds an artificial peak and a demo notice. This is a manual simulation.
  3. Record a trial. Open Experiment. After 5 seconds of baseline, mark Touch or Sham, then wait 10 seconds.
  4. Keep the evidence. Stop &download saves a ZIP with samples, events, calibration and labelled trial files.

When the hardware arrives

Open the hosted app in desktop Chrome on your Mac, upload the matching board firmware, then choose Connect USB and grant access to your board. Real board compatibility and signal quality still need testing.

USB always uses full timers: 120 seconds for dry soil and 30 seconds for a sustained moisture rise. Calibrate before relying on care notices.

This version provides in-app notices and optional desktop alerts while the page is open. Phone push notifications, background monitoring and a trained AI model are later work.
